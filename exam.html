The Python part

Two kinds of question, as in WISEflow. One correct answer: most show a few lines of code and ask what they produce. Tick every true statement: four statements about a piece of data work, of which one or more are true. The skills are the ones you practise in the labs, done in your head, and one or two questions are on the readings.

Sample exam for the Python part: ten questions with answers and explanations.

Best preparation: do each session’s self-test without AI, predict before you run, and redo the lab tasks from scratch.
